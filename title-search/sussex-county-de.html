What a Sussex County search covers

  • Current owner and vesting deed, read in full, with the legal description checked against the plat
  • Chain of title for a full search, or the two-owner period lenders and underwriters ask for
  • Open deeds of trust, each matched to a recorded release or listed as open
  • Judgments and liens against every owner in the period — Superior Court judgment records, federal and state tax liens, mechanics' liens
  • Easements, restrictions and covenants that touch the lot
  • Real estate tax status and prior-year payment history from the county finance or receiver of taxes office

Where the records are

Delaware has three counties, each with its own Recorder of Deeds. Tax parcel numbers are county specific, and county and school taxes are checked with the county that bills them. For Sussex County, land records are recorded with the Sussex County Recorder of Deeds, Georgetown.

Areas covered in Sussex County

Questions

How fast is a title search in Sussex County?
Most Sussex County searches are back in 4 to 48 hours from the order. Same-day rush is available when the records allow.
Where are Sussex County land records searched?
Deeds, deeds of trust, releases and plats are searched in the land records of the Sussex County Recorder of Deeds, Georgetown. Judgments are checked in the Superior Court judgment records, and taxes with the county finance or receiver of taxes office.
What does a Sussex County title search cost?
A pre-listing title search is $100, prepaid. Full, two-owner and document searches are quoted by reply, before any work starts.
Who orders from TRT?
Lenders, title agencies, closing attorneys and real estate agents. Each order comes back as a one-page abstract with copies of the recorded documents attached.
