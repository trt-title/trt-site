What a City of Chesapeake search covers

  • Current owner and vesting deed, read in full, with the legal description checked against the plat
  • Chain of title for a full search, or the two-owner period lenders and underwriters ask for
  • Open deeds of trust, each matched to a recorded release or listed as open
  • Judgments and liens against every owner in the period — circuit and general district court judgment dockets, federal and state tax liens, mechanics' liens
  • Easements, restrictions and covenants that touch the lot
  • Real estate tax status and prior-year payment history from the county or city treasurer

Where the records are

Virginia land records are kept by the Clerk of the Circuit Court in each county and independent city, and judgments are docketed in the same clerk's office. Independent cities such as Alexandria keep their own records apart from the surrounding county, so the jurisdiction matters before the search starts. For City of Chesapeake, land records are recorded with the Chesapeake Circuit Court, Chesapeake.
