What a Jefferson County search covers

  • Current owner and vesting deed, read in full, with the legal description checked against the plat
  • Chain of title for a full search, or the two-owner period lenders and underwriters ask for
  • Open deeds of trust, each matched to a recorded release or listed as open
  • Judgments and liens against every owner in the period — county clerk judgment lien records, federal and state tax liens, mechanics' liens
  • Easements, restrictions and covenants that touch the lot
  • Real estate tax status and prior-year payment history from the county sheriff's tax office

Where the records are

West Virginia land records, deeds of trust and judgment liens are kept by the County Clerk, and real estate taxes are collected by the county sheriff. Many older instruments are indexed by book and page only, which is where hands-on courthouse experience counts. For Jefferson County, land records are recorded with the Jefferson County Clerk, Charles Town.
