Services

What we search

I

Full & current-owner searches

Chain of title back to the vesting deed, with open deeds of trust, easements, and restrictions noted and abstracted.

II

Two-owner searches

The standard lender and underwriter request, run to the required period and delivered in the same format as a full search.

III

Deed & document retrieval

Recorded deeds, deeds of trust, plats, and releases pulled from county land records and delivered as clean, legible copies.

IV

Pre-listing title search

Run before the property hits the market, so a lien, judgment, or clouded chain surfaces while there is still time to clear it — not days before closing. Prepaid, for listing agents.

V

Tax status, liens & judgments

Real estate tax status including prior years' payment history, plus judgment and lien searches against the named parties.
